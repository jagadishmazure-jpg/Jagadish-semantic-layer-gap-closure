# ADR 0005: Proceed requires positive evidence

Status: accepted

**Decision.** Absence of a problem is not enough. Proceed needs an exact, factual, approved clearance term or a valid structured reading.

**Consequences.** It costs proceed recall in domains without clearance terms when no reading is supplied. In the ablation, turning it off added only 1 unsafe proceed on test because other rules overlap. It is kept as defence in depth.
